A sustainable weekly rhythm

  • Monday: mathematical reasoning (25 minutes)
  • Tuesday: verbal and vocabulary (25 minutes)
  • Wednesday: rest
  • Thursday: reading and language (25 minutes)
  • Friday: quick quiz (20 minutes)
  • Saturday: review errors and occasional longer simulation
  • Sunday: rest

What scores mean

  • App percentages are practice accuracy, not standardized scores or percentiles.
  • The strongest indicator is improving performance on new, unseen questions.
  • Distinguish missed concepts from misreading, calculation errors, or time pressure.
  • Any blank answer is counted as incorrect for practice scoring. There is no wrong-answer penalty on the HSPT, so an educated guess beats a blank.
  • Skill data from fewer than 10–15 recent questions are provisional.
  • Writing is recorded separately and requires a human reviewer.
